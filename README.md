# Beautiflized Modpack

## Overview
**Beautiflized** is a carefully curated Minecraft modpack designed to strike the perfect balance between breathtaking visuals and uncompromising performance. According to its core summary, it aims to make Minecraft look beautiful while having good performance. It achieves this by combining modern rendering engines with dynamic aesthetic tweaks.

## Key Mod Categories

### 🚀 Performance & Optimization
To ensure the game runs smoothly even with massive visual enhancements, the modpack includes a robust suite of optimization mods designed to minimize CPU and RAM usage while boosting framerates:
- **Sodium & Sodium Extra:** A modern rendering engine and UI improvements that vastly increase framerates and smooth out stuttering.
- **Lithium & Krypton:** Server-side optimizations targeting general tick performance, AI processing, and networking.
- **FerriteCore & MemoryLeakFix:** Significantly reduces RAM usage and prevents memory leaks over extended play sessions.
- **EntityCulling:** Improves performance by skipping the rendering of entities and block entities that are not currently visible to the player.
- **ModernFix & BadOptimizations:** Patches various bugs, improves game load times, and fixes underlying vanilla inefficiencies.
- **ImmediatelyFast:** Speeds up immediate mode rendering, particularly for UI elements and text.
- **C2ME:** Optimizes and parallelizes chunk generation.
- **Dynamic FPS:** Reduces resource usage when the Minecraft window is in the background.

### 🌟 Visuals & Aesthetics
This modpack serves as a complete visual overhaul, aiming to make your game look stunning without relying on outdated loaders:
- **Iris Shaders:** Allows for the seamless use of modern shader packs on the Fabric loader.
- **Entity Texture Features (ETF) & Entity Model Features (EMF):** Provides full support for custom OptiFine-style entity models and textures, enabling complex mob appearances.
- **Continuity:** Provides connected textures for blocks like glass, requiring no additional setup.
- **Visuality:** Adds beautiful ambient particle effects (e.g., slime particles, water splashes, and block interactions).
- **LambDynamicLights & DynamicLights:** Introduces dynamic lighting, allowing held items like torches and glowing entities to naturally illuminate the environment.
- **NotEnoughAnimations:** Adds missing animations to the player model, such as eating, drinking, and holding maps.
- **Capes & WaveyCapes:** Renders player capes with fluid, wavy physics for a much more realistic look.
- **Zoomify:** A high-quality zoom mod with smooth transitions, akin to traditional OptiFine zoom but highly configurable.

### 🛠️ Quality of Life & UI Enhancements
- **AppleSkin:** Displays accurate food and saturation values directly on your HUD.
- **ShulkerBoxTooltip:** Allows you to view the exact contents of shulker boxes directly in your inventory without having to place them down.
- **Clumps:** Groups experience orbs together into a single entity to reduce lag and make gathering XP significantly easier.
- **BetterF3 & Debugify:** Cleans up the cluttered F3 debug screen and squashes various lingering vanilla bugs.
- **ModMenu:** Provides an intuitive in-game screen to view and configure all installed mods.
- **VisualWorkbench:** Items stay securely in the crafting table when you close it, and are rendered as 3D items resting on top of the block.
- **Essential:** Adds major multiplayer and social features, including the ability to host single-player worlds and invite friends without needing a dedicated server.
- **EnchantmentDescriptions:** Adds highly helpful tooltips explaining exactly what each enchantment does.

### 🎨 Included Resource Packs
The pack comes pre-loaded with a carefully selected variety of resource packs to immediately enhance the look and feel of the game:
- **FreshAnimations:** Completely overhauls mob animations to be dynamic and highly expressive.
- **Better-Leaves:** Makes tree leaves appear bushy and full, breaking up the blocky nature of standard trees.
- **Simple Grass Flowers & Better+Lanterns:** Small but impactful environmental improvements to flora and lighting fixtures.
- **Fresh Food:** Revamped, appetizing textures for consumable items.
- **Enchantment Outlines & Icons:** Subtle UI and visual tweaks to make inventories look cleaner.
- **Cobblemon Interface Modded:** Custom UI elements for those integrating Cobblemon.
- **Low Shield & LowOnFire:** Adjusts obstructive visual elements, lowering the shield and fire height for better visibility during combat.
- *Note: Faithful 64x is included in the modpack files but is disabled by default.*
